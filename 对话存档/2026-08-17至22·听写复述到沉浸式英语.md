## ✉️ 邮件写作 Write an Email

### 5 大话题类别（每类练 ≥3 篇，考场归类直接套）

| 类别 | 典型场景 |
|---|---|
| ① 请求 / 资讯类 | 选课、请求讲义/文献 |
| ② 问题 / 处理类 | 作业、上传文件求助 |
| ③ 建议 / 推荐类 | 社团、推荐招新计划 |
| ④ 邀请 / 组织类 | 组员、开会时间地点 |
| ⑤ 感谢 / 跟进类 | 投稿、感谢校对发表 |

### 7 步骨架（基础版）

1. **称呼**：Dear [Name],
2. **问候**：I hope you are doing well.
3. **任务 1**（对齐题目 bullet 1）
4. **任务 2**（对齐 bullet 2）
5. **任务 3**（对齐 bullet 3）
6. **感谢**：Thank you very much for your time and assistance.
7. **落款**：Best regards, / [Your Name]

> 核心：**任务 1/2/3 逐条对齐题目的三个 bullet，绝不漏点。**

### 基础版 vs 高分升级版（同骨架，升级在血肉）

**基础版**（poetry 投稿情景）：
> Dear Editor,
> I hope you are doing well. I recently enjoyed reading the new issue and appreciated the variety of voices featured in the poems. I am writing to contact you regarding my recent poetry submissions to your magazine. However, I experienced a problem while using the online submission form. The page did not confirm whether my two poems were successfully submitted. Could you please let me know if my submissions were received? Thank you very much for your time and assistance.
> Best regards, Your Name

**高分升级版**（同题）：
> Dear Editor,
> I hope you are doing well. I really enjoyed the latest issue of Sunshine Poetry Magazine, especially the mix of styles and the way you highlight new writers. I am writing to follow up on two poems I submitted through your online form today around 3 p.m. After I uploaded both files and clicked "Submit," the page froze and kept loading without showing a confirmation message or email receipt. I tried refreshing the page and checking my inbox and spam folder, but I still cannot tell whether the submission went through. I'm excited to be considered and want to make sure everything is in order. Could you please confirm whether you received my poems (titles: River Lights and Winter Station), and let me know if you need me to resubmit them by email or through the form again? Thank you very much for your time and assistance.
> Best regards,

**升级点（骨架不变，细节加分）**：
- **① 具体化**——杂志名（Sunshine）、时间（3 p.m.）、诗名（River Lights / Winter Station）、试过的排查（刷新、查 spam）
- **② 更主动周到**——"want to make sure everything is in order"、给对方**两条解决路径**（email or form）
- **③ 更长更连贯**——句间有逻辑推进

→ **band 4→5 的差别 = 更具体 + 更周到，不是换结构。**
