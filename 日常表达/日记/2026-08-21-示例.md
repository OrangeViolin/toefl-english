# 2026-08-21 · 示例：What did I spend the most time on today?

> 这是一篇**示例**，演示归档格式。你真正写的时候，对 cc 说 `英语日记：<你的英文>` 即可，cc 会自动生成下面这三段。

## 原文（我写的）

Today I spend most of time on speaking practice. I did four speaking mock exams. I think speaking is my weakest part, so I want to practice more. But I sleep too late again, almost 2 o'clock. I know it is not good for my body.

## cc 批改

- 🟢 **敢写、写满**：一口气讲清了"做了什么 + 为什么 + 反思"，结构完整，这就是好的输出习惯。
- 🟡 **时态**：讲今天已发生的事用一般过去时——`spend → spent`、`do → did`（你后面 did 就对了）、`sleep → slept`。
- 🟡 **地道搭配**：`spend most of time` → **`spent most of my time`**（time 前要 my）；`my weakest part` → **`my weakest area / my weak spot`**。
- 🔴 **中式直译**：`not good for my body` → native 更说 **`bad for my health`**。

**地道化重写**：
> Today I **spent most of my time** on speaking practice — I did four speaking mock exams. Speaking is **my weakest area**, so I want to drill it more. But I **stayed up too late again**, almost 2 a.m., which I know is **bad for my health**.

## 抽取

- **表达/句型** → 写作表达库：
  - `spend most of one's time on sth`（把大部分时间花在…）
  - `stay up too late`（熬夜到很晚）— 比 sleep late 地道
  - `drill sth`（反复操练某项技能）
- **生词** → 背词计划（src_from「日常表达·日记」）：
  - `drill` /drɪl/ v. 反复操练
