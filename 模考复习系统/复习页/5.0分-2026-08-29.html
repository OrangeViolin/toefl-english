🎧 完全听不懂时·边听边抓（尤其学术讲座）——尽量多理解的 5 步
① 开头一句定主题：学术讲座第一句几乎必点主题——"Today we'll focus on X" / "Let's talk about X"。抓住 X 是什么，后面全围着它转。细节没听懂，至少知道在讲什么，主旨题就能拿下。
② 顺信号词拼骨架（配合上面🚦分色高亮）：
  • 🔴 but/however/instead → 前面被转折/否定，后面才是重点
  • 🔵 because/so/therefore → 因果，抓「因为什么 → 所以什么」
  • 🟢 for example/such as/called → 要举例或下定义了，例子帮你理解前面的抽象点；"a X called Y" 必考 Y 是什么
  • 🟣 first/then/also/another → 在列点，心里数 1、2、3
  • 🟠 the key/importantly/actually → 划重点，这句最可能考
③ 抓实词、放虚词：名词+动词是内容，介词冠词不用抠。某句没听懂就别卡住——等下一个信号词/例子，它常会换句话再解释一遍。
④ 重复 = 重点：反复出现的词（stalagmite、algal bloom…）就是核心概念，围着它记；数字/人名先记下，但「为什么提它」比它本身更重要。
⑤ 学术讲座万能骨架：现象/定义 → 原因/机制 → 例子/证据 → 影响/结论。就算只抓到「讲某现象、有原因、举了例、最后有个启示」，也够答对主旨题 + 修辞目的题 + 推断题（占大头）。

Today, we're revisiting the topic of urban green spaces, green areas in cities like parks and gardens. These spaces are known to improve air quality and reduce heat in cities. They also support the health of city residents. Creating and maintaining urban green spaces can be challenging, however, especially in densely populated cities. An obvious issue is space. In areas where land is expensive or already developed, finding room for parks or gardens can be difficult. One way cities address this is by building green roofs. That's a layer of vegetation planted on top of a building. Green roofs help cool buildings, absorb rainwater, and add greenery without taking up ground space. Cities also often repurpose unused lots, such as abandoned parking areas or vacant lots, turning them into small parks or gardens. Vertical gardens are another innovation. These are walls covered with plants, often using special structures that allow greenery to grow upward. They're useful in places where horizontal space is limited and can improve air quality and insulation. While cost and upkeep of green spaces are legitimate concerns, many cities are adopting green space quotas, policies that require a minimum amount of greenery in new developments. These quotas mandate builders to include features such as trees, rooftop gardens, or courtyards. Though they may raise construction costs, urban planners support them because they ensure that green space is not overlooked in city growth. Quotas help balance development with environmental health and are a step toward more sustainable urban living.

Another risk is loss of control. Once content goes viral, it is no longer in the hands of the brand. Memes, remixes, and commentary can distort the original message, sometimes in ways that conflict with the brand's values or goals. Despite these risks, viral marketing remains an invaluable tool, particularly for startups and emerging brands seeking rapid traction. ✓John Cage & Prepared Piano · 预制钢琴（2/4 ❌错2）2/4⏱ 3m24s

Imagine taking a regular piano and transforming its sound by inserting everyday objects like screws, bolts, and rubber erasers between the strings. This is what's known as a prepared piano. It was developed by the avant garde composer John Cage in 1940. Cage was working at a university in Seattle, and he needed to write music with percussion to accompany dancers. But the performance space's stage wasn't big enough for percussion instruments like drums. So he made the sounds with just the piano by placing objects between the strings. Now, Cage spent time searching for items that made the kind of sounds he was looking for. It had a feel of improvisation. Cage himself said that looking for items was kind of like walking on the beach and looking for shiny or beautiful seashells that you liked. That is, he didn't know ahead of time what items he wanted to use. He just kept looking around and seeing what suited his needs. Cage ended up composing a collection of music for prepared piano in the mid 1940s. He included specific instructions for how to prepare the piano. For this collection, Cage was inspired by ancient Hindu theory and about eight essential emotions. He wanted his listeners to sense eight essential emotions from Hindu philosophy. The result is a blending of sounds that would be familiar to a Western audience, plus sounds reminiscent of Indonesian gamelan. Gamelan is traditional Indonesian percussion music. This prepared piano music really raised John Cage's profile in the music world.

  • 🔴 but/however/instead → 前面被转折/否定，后面才是重点
