① 开头一句定主题：学术讲座第一句几乎必点主题——"Today we'll focus on X" / "Let's talk about X"。抓住 X 是什么，后面全围着它转。细节没听懂，至少知道在讲什么，主旨题就能拿下。
② 顺信号词拼骨架（配合上面🚦分色高亮）：
  • 🔴 but/however/instead → 前面被转折/否定，后面才是重点
  • 🔵 because/so/therefore → 因果，抓「因为什么 → 所以什么」
  • 🟢 for example/such as/called → 要举例或下定义了，例子帮你理解前面的抽象点；"a X called Y" 必考 Y 是什么
  • 🟣 first/then/also/another → 在列点，心里数 1、2、3
  • 🟠 the key/importantly/actually → 划重点，这句最可能考
③ 抓实词、放虚词：名词+动词是内容，介词冠词不用抠。某句没听懂就别卡住——等下一个信号词/例子，它常会换句话再解释一遍。
④ 重复 = 重点：反复出现的词（stalagmite、algal bloom…）就是核心概念，围着它记；数字/人名先记下，但「为什么提它」比它本身更重要。
⑤ 学术讲座万能骨架：现象/定义 → 原因/机制 → 例子/证据 → 影响/结论。就算只抓到「讲某现象、有原因、举了例、最后有个启示」，也够答对主旨题 + 修辞目的题 + 推断题（占大头）。

✓Bird Migration 讲座（2/4 ❌错2）2/4
🎧 听力原文 · 点词展开 ·
Let's talk a bit about bird migration. Migratory birds travel vast distances between their breeding and wintering grounds, often covering thousands of miles. These journeys are driven by the need to find suitable habitats with adequate food supplies and favorable climate conditions. One of the most remarkable aspects of migration is a bird's ability to navigate accurately over such long distances. Birds use a variety of navigational cues, such as the position of the sun, stars, and, incredibly, Earth's magnetic field. Certain bird species have a special protein in their eyes that allows them to see magnetic fields, and this helps them stay on course. Migratory birds play key roles in ecosystems by dispersing seeds and controlling pests, so their decline can cause imbalances throughout food webs. Of course, a migration over hundreds or even thousands of miles will be fraught with risk. Obvious dangers include harsh weather conditions, predators, and the risk of exhaustion and starvation. Less obviously, human activities also pose significant threats to migratory birds. Habitat destruction, pollution, and climate change have led to a decline in many migratory bird populations. At night, light pollution can disorient birds that navigate by the stars, causing them to become lost or collide with buildings.

✓Lean Manufacturing 讲座（2/4 ❌错2）2/4
🎧 听力原文 · 点词展开 ·
Let's talk about lean manufacturing and its impact on efficiency in production processes. Lean manufacturing is a systematic approach to minimizing waste within a manufacturing system while maximizing productivity. While the methodology has been adopted by many companies worldwide, it was developed by a Japanese carmaker in the mid 20th century. But its roots can actually be traced to the supermarket. In the 1950s, Japanese engineers observed that supermarkets in the United States only restocked items as customers purchased them, maintaining just enough inventory to meet demand. This idea of just in time inventory management became a core principle of lean manufacturing. By identifying and removing excess inventory and eliminating other inefficiencies, companies could reduce costs and increase their overall productivity. Another important aspect of lean manufacturing is continuous improvement, often referred to as kaizen. This involves constantly seeking ways to enhance processes, products and services. Companies at all levels are encouraged to contribute ideas for improvement, fostering a culture of innovation and teamwork. Lean manufacturing also emphasizes the importance of value from the customer's perspective. Companies focus on producing goods that meet customer needs and expectations, ensuring that each step in the production process adds value rather than unnecessary complexity. As you might imagine, implementing lean manufacturing requires a shift in organizational culture. Resistance to change and difficulty in sustaining improvements can get in the way. That said, companies that successfully adopt lean practices experience increased efficiency, reduced costs and higher customer satisfaction.
