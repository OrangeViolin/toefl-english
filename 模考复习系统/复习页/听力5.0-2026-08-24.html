  • 修辞目的题已过关：青蛙 Q4(蜜蜂)、城市热岛 Q3(树)都对，稳定用『这句服务哪个论点』的思路，保持。
🛠 补强处方
  • 对话首要担忧题：锁『说话人最先、最主动抛出的那件事』，后文的 app/新机/工作都是被它牵出来的后续，不是担忧本身；答案词多在开头 15 秒。
  • 讲座定义/细节：听到 a X called Y、Y is a X、must/above 数字、evenly/symmetrical 这类词立刻在脑内标记『这是定义/硬指标』，边听边默念 chytrid=真菌，防止把术语和它攻击的对象(青蛙)搞混、防止跨篇串味(城市热岛的『城市』别飘到青蛙题)。
  • 修辞目的题维持现有打法：先判断被提到的例子(蜜蜂/树)是在支持哪个论点(指示物/降温)，不要停在字面比较或因果误读。
🎧 完全听不懂时·边听边抓（尤其学术讲座）——尽量多理解的 5 步
① 开头一句定主题：学术讲座第一句几乎必点主题——"Today we'll focus on X" / "Let's talk about X"。抓住 X 是什么，后面全围着它转。细节没听懂，至少知道在讲什么，主旨题就能拿下。
② 顺信号词拼骨架（配合上面🚦分色高亮）：
  • 🔴 but/however/instead → 前面被转折/否定，后面才是重点
  • 🔵 because/so/therefore → 因果，抓「因为什么 → 所以什么」
  • 🟢 for example/such as/called → 要举例或下定义了，例子帮你理解前面的抽象点；"a X called Y" 必考 Y 是什么
  • 🟣 first/then/also/another → 在列点，心里数 1、2、3
  • 🟠 the key/importantly/actually → 划重点，这句最可能考
③ 抓实词、放虚词：名词+动词是内容，介词冠词不用抠。某句没听懂就别卡住——等下一个信号词/例子，它常会换句话再解释一遍。
④ 重复 = 重点：反复出现的词（stalagmite、algal bloom…）就是核心概念，围着它记；数字/人名先记下，但「为什么提它」比它本身更重要。
⑤ 学术讲座万能骨架：现象/定义 → 原因/机制 → 例子/证据 → 影响/结论。就算只抓到「讲某现象、有原因、举了例、最后有个启示」，也够答对主旨题 + 修辞目的题 + 推断题（占大头）。

✓学术讲座 · 陶器的发展（3/4 · 错Q2）3/4⏱ 约2分
🎧 听力原文 · 点词展开 ·
Previously, we discussed the significance of pottery to ancient cultures, including pottery as an art form and pottery's crucial role in trade. Today, we'll focus on the development of pottery over time. One breakthrough was the invention of the potter's wheel, around 3500 BCE in Mesopotamia. Before this, pottery was shaped mostly by hand, which was slow and produced less uniform results. The potter's wheel allowed artisans to spin and shape the clay more quickly and evenly, making it possible to create symmetrical vessels with greater precision and speed. Firing technology also advanced. Early pots were fired in open pits, which limited temperature control, resulting in uneven or fragile pots. So kilns were developed, enclosed ovens that could reach higher and more consistent temperatures. These allowed for stronger, more durable pottery. Another advance was the updraft kiln, which directed heat upward through the chamber. By adjusting the airflow, potters could control how much oxygen reached the fire. This helped them produce pottery with specific colors like red or black, depending on how the clay reacted to the firing atmosphere. Then, in ancient China, potters developed kilns that could reach very high temperatures. This allowed them to create stoneware, and eventually porcelain. Porcelain is made from a special mix of fine clay and minerals, and must be fired at temperatures above 1,200 degrees Celsius. The result is a smooth, white ceramic that is strong, lightweight, and slightly translucent. Porcelain was a major breakthrough because it was more durable and versatile than earlier pottery.

Frogs aren't just their croaks and leaps. They're key players in ecosystems, acting as both predators and prey. But scientists have been sounding the alarm. Frog populations are shrinking fast across the globe. One major reason is chemical pollution. Pesticides and other pollutants often end up in the water where frogs live and breed. It's one of the most devastating wildlife diseases ever recorded. Why is this problematic? Frogs also serve as indicators of environmental health. Because they're so sensitive to changes in water quality and temperature, their decline often signals deeper ecological problems. Bees play a similar role. When bee populations drop, it can point to issues like pesticide overuse or habitat stress. So when frogs and bees are struggling, it's a warning sign that the environment needs attention.

✓学术讲座 · 城市热岛（3/4 · 错Q2）3/4⏱ 约1分
🎧 听力原文 · 点词展开 ·
Today I'd like to discuss urban heat islands. Cities often become warmer than surrounding rural areas because pavement, rooftops, and buildings absorb and retain heat during the day, then release it slowly at night. Trees and other vegetation usually cool an area through shade and evaporation, but cities often have less plant cover. Human activity also contributes: vehicles, air conditioners, and industrial equipment all release additional heat. This matters because higher temperatures can increase energy use, worsen air pollution, and create health risks during heat waves. One effective response is to add green roofs and plant more trees, which lower surface temperatures and provide shade. Another is to use lighter colored roofing materials that reflect more sunlight instead of absorbing it. None of these strategies eliminates urban heat entirely, but together they can make cities noticeably cooler and more comfortable.
🇨🇳 听力原文翻译（先自己读，点开中英对照）
今天我想讨论一下城市热岛。城市往往会比周边的乡村地区更热，因为路面、屋顶和建筑物在白天吸收并储存热量，然后在夜间缓慢地把热量释放出来。树木和其他植被通常能通过遮阴和蒸发来给一个区域降温，但城市往往植被覆盖较少。人类活动也是原因之一：车辆、空调和工业设备都会释放出额外的热量。这之所以重要，是因为温度升高会增加能源消耗、加剧空气污染，并在热浪期间带来健康风险。一种有效的应对办法是增设绿色屋顶、多种树木，这样能降低地表温度并提供遮阴。另一种办法是使用颜色较浅的屋顶材料，它们能反射更多阳光，而不是把阳光吸收进来。这些策略没有一种能彻底消除城市的热量，但它们结合起来，就能让城市明显变得更凉爽、更舒适。
