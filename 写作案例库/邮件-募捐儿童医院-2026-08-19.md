# 邮件案例 · 募捐儿童医院 · 2026-08-19

> 类别：邮件·请求/邀请类 ｜ 用时：6min46s ｜ cc 评估原答案 ≈ band 3

## 1. 题目

A local community organization is planning a fundraising event for a local children's hospital. You want to contact Ms. Lopez to discuss the event and possible support.

Write an email to Ms. Lopez. In your email, do the following.
1. Describe the purpose of the fundraising event.
2. Explain why you think the activity will be successful.
3. Suggest a time to meet and discuss the plans in more detail.

*Write as much as you can and in complete sentences. (7 minutes)*

## 2. 我的原答案（band ≈ 3）

> Dear Ms.Lopez
>
> I would like to discuss with you about the local children's hospital fundraising which can benefit the community deeply. Everybody know the importance of the event, so it will be successful nodoultly. I have work in both hospital and funding, can we meet this weekend and disuss the plans in more detail. Hopefully to offer some help.
>
> yours

**问题**：拼写 nodoultly→no doubt、disuss→discuss；语法 Everybody know→knows、I have work→I have worked、discuss ~~with you about~~→discuss…with you；结构 "为什么成功"太空、run-on 逗号粘连；语域 yours→Best regards。

## 3. 其他 AI 润色版（~150 词，展开充分但对我的打字速度不现实）

> Dear Ms. Lopez, I am writing to discuss an upcoming fundraising event for our local children's hospital. This initiative aims to provide essential medical resources and support for families in need within our community. I am very optimistic that this activity will be successful because there is already a strong sense of collective responsibility among local residents. Everyone understands how vital the hospital's services are, so I expect a high level of participation. Additionally, my background in both healthcare and financial planning allows me to offer unique insights into making this event effective. I would love to meet with you this weekend to discuss these plans in more detail and explore how we can work together. Please let me know if you have a preferred time to talk on Saturday or Sunday. I look forward to hearing your thoughts on this meaningful project. Best regards, Student

> Dear Ms. Lopez,
>
> I am writing to discuss a fundraising event for our local children's hospital, which aims to provide medical resources for families in need. I am optimistic it will succeed, because our community strongly supports the hospital. My background in healthcare and finance could also help. Could we meet this weekend to discuss the plans in more detail? Please let me know a time that works for you.
>
> Best regards,
> [Name]
